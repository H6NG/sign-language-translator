# How It Works — Competitor 1: Dark Fintech App (Receipt/Invoice Tool)

## Overview

A dark-themed "How it works" section from a fintech/accounting product. Uses a **split layout** — text steps on the left, a live product UI mockup on the right.

---

## Layout

- **Two-column split**: left ~40% for text steps, right ~60% for product mockup
- The mockup is a full UI screenshot showing an inbox of receipts — it acts as visual proof of the feature being described
- Left column is vertically stacked text items, one highlighted/active at a time
- The active step has bold white text + a short description paragraph beneath it; inactive steps are **dimmed / muted grey** text only
- Feels like a **scrollable or click-to-advance** walkthrough — only one step is "expanded" at a time

## Typography

- **Section title ("How it works")**: medium-large, light serif or semibold sans-serif, white, left-aligned
- **Step titles**: ~18–20px, white when active, medium-grey when inactive
- **Step description** (active only): ~14px, lighter grey, 1–2 lines max
- Clear weight hierarchy: title > active step heading > description > inactive steps

## Color

- **Background**: near-black / very dark charcoal (#0d0d0d or similar)
- **Text**: white for active elements, medium-grey (#888–#aaa) for inactive
- All monochrome — no accent color visible in this section
- Product mockup on the right also dark-themed, maintaining visual cohesion

## Spacing

- Generous vertical gaps between step items (~40–60px)
- The section itself has large top/bottom padding (~100–120px)
- Left text column has a visual "bar" or indicator next to the active step (small square dot)

## Patterns & Principles

- **Progressive disclosure**: only one step is visually "open" at a time — the others are collapsed labels
- **Product-as-proof**: the right column shows the actual UI, synced to whichever step is active
- **Minimal interactivity cue**: the small square indicator next to the active step is the only visual signal of state
- **Dark-on-dark cohesion**: the product mockup is rendered in the same dark palette as the section background, feeling embedded rather than screenshot-pasted
- Navigation bar at top is minimal: logo left, text links centered, "Sign in" on the right — all in muted white/grey

## Key Takeaways

1. Split layout with synced mockup is highly effective — "show, don't tell"
2. Progressive disclosure keeps the section clean even with multiple steps
3. Monochrome palette avoids visual noise — the content structure does the work
4. Active/inactive contrast is the main visual hierarchy tool
