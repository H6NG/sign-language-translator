# How It Works — Competitor 2: Framer Teams

## Overview

Dark-themed "How it works" section from Framer's Teams page. Uses a **two-column layout** — title + subtitle + CTA on the left, numbered steps with icons on the right.

---

## Layout

- **Two-column asymmetric split**: left ~35% for the section heading, right ~65% for the step content
- Left column is **sticky/fixed** in feel — holds the section title, a short subtitle, and a CTA button
- Right column has **3 steps stacked vertically** with generous spacing between them
- Each step: **colored circular icon** → **bold heading** → **description paragraph** (all left-aligned within the right column)
- Steps are not numbered with digits — they rely on vertical order and icon differentiation

## Typography

- **Section title ("How it works")**: very large, ~40–48px, bold, white, serif or display sans-serif
- **Subtitle**: ~16px, regular weight, light grey, 2–3 lines
- Key phrase in subtitle is **bold/white** ("Scale plan") for emphasis
- **Step headings**: ~22–24px, bold, white
- **Step descriptions**: ~14–15px, regular, medium grey (#aaa–#bbb), 2–3 lines max

## Color

- **Background**: dark/near-black
- **Text**: white for headings, medium grey for body
- **Accent**: **bright blue** circular icons next to each step — this is the only color in the entire section
- The icons contain white arrow/symbol inside a solid blue (#007AFF-style) filled circle
- CTA button: **white text on dark with a white border** (outlined pill shape), not filled

## Spacing

- Very generous vertical gaps between steps (~80–100px)
- Large section padding (~120px top/bottom)
- Left column content is vertically centered relative to the right column content
- Step descriptions indented/aligned with the heading, offset from the icon

## Patterns & Principles

- **Left-right narrative flow**: the left sets up the "what" and "why," the right delivers the "how"
- **Vertical step list** (not a grid) — reads like a story, top to bottom
- **Single accent color** for icons creates visual rhythm without noise
- **CTA placement**: the "Schedule kickoff" button lives in the left column alongside the section context — encourages action without interrupting the step flow
- **No numbers**: steps are implied by vertical order + distinct icons, keeping it clean
- **Minimal decoration**: no borders, no cards, no shadows — just spacing and typography

## Key Takeaways

1. Two-column layout with sticky context (left) + scrollable steps (right) is elegant and readable
2. Single accent color on icons creates strong visual anchoring for each step
3. Vertical stacking with extreme spacing makes each step feel like its own moment
4. CTA embedded in the context column (not after the steps) is a smart placement
5. No cards or containers — pure whitespace-driven structure
