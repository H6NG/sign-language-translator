# Competitor FAQ Analysis: Shuttle (Light Theme)

## Overview

A clean, light-themed FAQ section from the Shuttle file-sharing product. Minimalist design with accordion-style questions.

---

## Color Usage

- **Background**: Light grey / off-white (`~#f5f5f5`)
- **Card/accordion items**: White cards on the light grey background — subtle distinction
- **Text**: Black for the section heading and questions, dark grey for answer body text
- **CTA button**: Solid black pill button with white text ("Contact us →")
- **Chevron icons**: Light grey/muted, right-aligned on each question row

## Typography

- **Section title ("FAQ")**: Very large, bold, black, sans-serif — centered, likely 48–56px. All caps or natural case
- **Subtitle**: Medium grey, standard sans-serif ("Have a question? We have answers.")
- **Questions**: Regular weight sans-serif, ~16px, dark/black
- **Answers**: Regular weight, slightly smaller or same size, dark grey body text

## Layout & Spacing

- **Center-aligned**: Title, subtitle, and CTA centered above the accordion
- **Accordion container**: Centered, narrow max-width (~650px), with even spacing
- **Each accordion item**: Full-width within container, separated by subtle borders or gaps (~8–12px)
- **Expanded item**: Shows answer text indented below the question with comfortable padding
- **Vertical flow**: Title → Subtitle → CTA button → Accordion list
- **Bottom**: "Shuttle" branding and "curated by Mobbin" at page bottom

## Design Patterns

- **Accordion/expandable**: Click to expand answers — classic FAQ UX
- **Chevron toggle**: Small `v` chevron on the right side of each question
- **One item expanded**: Only one question is shown open at a time ("Can I cancel at anytime?" is expanded)
- **CTA above questions**: "Contact us →" button between the heading and the accordion — drives support contact
- **Minimal card styling**: Questions are in white containers with very subtle borders, no heavy shadows

## Key Takeaways

- Ultra-clean, no visual noise — just questions and answers
- White accordion cards on off-white background creates gentle depth
- CTA placed above the FAQ list, not buried at the bottom
- Chevron icons provide clear affordance for expandability
- Narrow, centered container keeps focus tight
