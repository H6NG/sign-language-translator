# Competitor FAQ Analysis: Dark Theme SaaS (Industrial/Manufacturing)

## Overview

A dark-themed FAQ section from what appears to be an industrial/manufacturing SaaS product. Uses dark accordion cards with a bold, direct tone.

---

## Color Usage

- **Background**: Dark grey / near-black for the accordion area, lighter background above for the heading
- **Accordion cards**: Very dark grey/charcoal cards with subtle borders or gaps between them
- **Text**: White for questions, lighter grey for answer body text
- **Accent**: Coral/salmon pink for the "Got Questions" badge above the title
- **Toggle icons**: `+` and `−` symbols on the right side of each card, in muted grey/white
- **Heading area**: Lighter background (white or off-white) for the title vs dark for the cards

## Typography

- **Badge ("Got Questions")**: Small, coral/salmon colored text — acts as a label/tag above the title
- **Section title ("FAQ")**: Very large, bold serif font — likely 48–56px. The "Q" appears stylized/decorative
- **Questions**: Bold or semi-bold sans-serif, white, ~16–18px — direct, conversational tone
- **Answers**: Regular weight sans-serif, lighter grey, standard body size — longer, detailed responses
- **Question tone**: Notably conversational/objection-handling: "We've been burned by software before...", "How do I justify this to my CEO/Board..."

## Layout & Spacing

- **Heading section**: Light background, centered — badge → title, clean vertical flow
- **Accordion section**: Dark background, full-width dark container wrapping the cards
- **Cards**: Stacked vertically with small gaps (~8–12px) between them
- **Expanded card**: First question is expanded with the answer visible, using a `−` icon
- **Collapsed cards**: Show only the question text with a `+` icon on the right
- **Generous internal padding**: Cards have ~20–24px padding

## Design Patterns

- **Badge/label above title**: "Got Questions" in accent color — provides context before the heading
- **Plus/minus toggle**: `+` for collapsed, `−` for expanded — alternative to chevrons
- **Dark card stack**: All cards share a dark background, creating a contained block
- **Objection-handling questions**: Questions are written from the customer's POV, addressing real concerns
- **Single expansion**: Only one item open at a time
- **Two-tone layout**: Light heading area transitioning to dark accordion area

## Key Takeaways

- The two-tone (light heading / dark cards) creates strong visual contrast and section definition
- Plus/minus toggles are a clean alternative to chevrons
- Writing questions from the user's perspective ("We've been burned...") feels more empathetic and personal
- The accent-colored badge above the title adds a touch of color without overwhelming
- Bold serif title with the stylized "Q" adds personality
- Dark cards with white text feel modern and work well for longer answer content
