# Common Design Patterns Across Competitors

A synthesis of recurring themes, principles, and patterns observed across all 5 competitor landing pages (Dovetail, SaaS Product Page, Ctrl Wallet, Lens Protocol, Popcorn).

---

## 1. Typography as the Primary Visual Tool

Every competitor relies on **large, bold headline typography** as the dominant visual element in the hero section.

| Competitor | Headline Style | Approx. Size |
|---|---|---|
| Dovetail | Bold serif | 60–80px+ |
| SaaS Page | Bold sans-serif | ~40–56px |
| Ctrl Wallet | Massive bold sans-serif/grotesque | 100–150px+ |
| Lens Protocol | Bold serif | ~48–56px |
| Popcorn | Bold serif | 56–72px |

**Key pattern**: 4 out of 5 use **serif fonts** for the hero headline — a modern editorial trend that conveys authority and premium quality. Body text universally switches to **clean sans-serif** for readability.

---

## 2. Extreme Whitespace / Generous Spacing

All competitors use **aggressive whitespace** as a deliberate design tool:

- Hero sections have **100–250px+** of vertical padding
- Content is **centered** and constrained to a narrow max-width container
- Elements within the hero are spaced **24–32px** apart vertically
- The overall impression is **airy, calm, and uncluttered**

**Principle**: Whitespace is treated as a design element, not empty space. It creates focus, hierarchy, and a premium feel.

---

## 3. Minimalist Color Palettes

Every competitor uses a **highly restrained color palette** — typically monochromatic with a single accent color:

| Competitor | Base | Accent Color | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dovetail | Black/white | Red/orange | Decorative lines |
| SaaS Page | White/grey | Purple/lavender | CTAs, gradients |
| Ctrl Wallet | White/black | Green | Single CTA button |
| Lens Protocol | White/black | Teal/mint-green | CTA, card accents |
| Popcorn | Off-white/black | Pink/coral | Glow effect, badge |

**Principle**: One accent color used sparingly creates a stronger focal point than multiple colors competing for attention. The accent color almost always highlights the **primary CTA**.

---

## 4. Navigation Patterns

A consistent nav structure appears across all competitors:

- **Left**: Logo + brand name
- **Center/Center-left**: Navigation links (3–5 items)
- **Right**: Primary CTA button (usually a **pill/rounded shape**)
- Several use **pill-shaped containers** or **rounded borders** around nav elements (Dovetail, Ctrl Wallet)
- Nav text is always **small, clean sans-serif**

**Principle**: Navigation is minimal and unobtrusive — it doesn't compete with the hero content.

---

## 5. Clear Content Hierarchy in the Hero

Every hero section follows a nearly identical **vertical flow**:

```
[Optional: Badge / Announcement]
         ↓
   [Main Headline]
         ↓
  [Subheadline / Description]
         ↓
    [CTA Button(s)]
         ↓
[Optional: Trust bar / Product preview]
```

- 3 out of 5 include a **small badge or announcement** above the headline (Dovetail's keynote, Lens's mascot, Popcorn's "Now live in Alpha!")
- All use **center-aligned** content in the hero
- Subheadlines are always **lighter weight, smaller, and greyed** compared to the headline

---

## 6. Dual CTA Pattern

Most competitors use a **primary + secondary CTA** pairing:

| Competitor | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Dovetail | "Try Dovetail free" (filled) | "Contact sales" (ghost/outlined) |
| Ctrl Wallet | "Download for Chrome" (green fill) | — (single CTA) |
| Lens Protocol | "Start Building" (teal fill) | "Developer Docs" (text link) |
| Popcorn | "Sign up" (dark fill) | — (single CTA) |

**Principle**: When two CTAs are present, there is always a clear visual hierarchy — one filled/colored, one outlined or text-only.

---

## 7. Trust & Social Proof

Multiple competitors include trust-building elements:

- **Dovetail**: Logo bar of major companies (AWS, Intel, Shopify, etc.)
- **SaaS Page**: Testimonials, user avatars, client logos
- **Ctrl Wallet**: Product UI preview below the fold
- **Lens Protocol**: Feature cards demonstrating platform capabilities
- **Popcorn**: Real device mockups showing the actual app

**Principle**: Trust is built through _showing_ (logos, real UI, device mockups) rather than just _telling_.

---

## 8. Product Visualization Below the Hero

Every competitor provides a **visual preview** of the product beneath or at the bottom of the hero:

- **Dovetail**: Decorative data-visualization grid
- **SaaS Page**: Product screenshots in alternating layouts
- **Ctrl Wallet**: Rounded-corner product UI card peeking from below the fold
- **Lens Protocol**: Feature card grid
- **Popcorn**: iPhone mockups showing the actual app UI with a warm glow effect

**Principle**: "Show, don't tell" — users want to see the product, not just read about it.

---

## 9. Rounded / Pill-Shaped UI Elements

Across all competitors, UI elements favor **soft, rounded shapes**:

- Pill-shaped CTA buttons
- Rounded nav containers
- Cards with rounded corners
- Badges with full border-radius

**Principle**: Sharp corners are avoided entirely. Rounded elements feel modern, friendly, and approachable.

---

## 10. Light/White as the Dominant Background

4 out of 5 competitors use a **white or off-white background**. Even Dovetail's dark theme is the exception that proves the rule — it uses the dark background as a deliberate contrast play for a specific brand identity.

**Principle**: Clean, light backgrounds remain the dominant trend for landing pages. They maximize readability, feel open, and let content breathe.

---

## Summary of Universal Principles

1. **Less is more** — every element must earn its place
2. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — especially large serif headlines
3. **One accent color, used sparingly** — for maximum impact on CTAs
4. **Whitespace is a feature** — generous spacing conveys premium quality
5. **Show the product** — mockups, screenshots, and real UI build trust
6. **Center-aligned hero with clear vertical hierarchy** — badge → headline → subtext → CTA
7. **Rounded shapes everywhere** — pills, rounded corners, soft edges
8. **Navigation stays out of the way** — minimal, clean, unobtrusive
